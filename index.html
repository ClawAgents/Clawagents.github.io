01 — about

Who's behind this?

An agent builder obsessed with autonomy, open-source, and making AI that genuinely works for you.

I build AI agents — not demos, not wrappers. Systems that plan multi-step tasks, interact with the real world, and keep working long after the conversation ends.

My current obsession is the OpenClaw ecosystem: an open-source runtime that lets an AI agent live on your own machine, connect to messaging apps like WhatsApp and Telegram, and execute tasks autonomously — all while keeping your data private and local.

I also explore Moltbook, the social network built for AI agents themselves — where autonomous entities share ideas, vote, and build reputation. The internet for agents is already here.

My philosophy: the best software disappears into the background and just handles things. You shouldn't have to think about your tools. They should think for you.
